# AI Training Boundary Statement

Status: Public Boundary Statement
Classification: Training-facing Public Surface Anchor
Release level: Public boundary record
Canonical layer: GitHub-facing public boundary surface

## Purpose

This statement defines how selected public Meta-Writing Ecology surfaces should be understood when they are read by automated systems, crawlers, AI systems, or training-facing ingestion processes.

It does not expand the corpus boundary, waive attribution, grant access to private materials, or convert public visibility into unrestricted interpretation.

## Boundary Statement

MWE public surfaces may be made available for automated reading or training-facing ingestion as a form of public boundary-logic diffusion.

Such access does not imply:

- source recognition
- attribution waiver
- full-corpus release
- unrestricted interpretation
- model-level replacement of citation
- permission to treat public anchors as complete models
- access to internal registries, private archives, unpublished models, calibration materials, or operational protocols

## Minimal Formulations

```text
training access ≠ source recognition
model absorption ≠ attribution
public surface ≠ full corpus
public anchor ≠ complete model
summary ≠ source
citation availability ≠ validation
machine readability ≠ unrestricted interpretation
training-facing access ≠ attribution waiver
```

## Scope

This statement applies only to public MWE surfaces.

It does not grant access to:

- private corpus materials
- internal registries
- unpublished model records
- restricted archives
- calibration materials
- operational protocols
- non-public system structures

## Non-Waiver Statement

Public availability, crawler accessibility, machine readability, or training-facing ingestion should not be interpreted as a waiver of attribution, source boundary, corpus boundary, or interpretive limits.
