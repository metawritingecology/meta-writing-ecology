# Source Boundary Statement

Meta-Writing Ecology public anchors are source-visible navigation records.

They do not represent the complete working corpus, full internal registry, private archive, or complete operational model layer.

A public anchor may provide:

- a public name
- classification status
- minimal formulation
- boundary note
- related terms
- source-navigation placement

A public anchor should not be treated as:

- the full model
- a complete protocol
- a final taxonomy
- a legal, medical, psychological, scientific, or policy claim
- a replacement for source-level reading
- a substitute for internal calibration

Public visibility does not equal full release.

Citation availability does not equal source completeness.

Summary access does not equal model authority.
