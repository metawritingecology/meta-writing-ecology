## Minimal Formulation

Reality Consistency is the structural condition in which an event, claim, media object, institutional output, or machine-generated result remains supportable as more dimensions of it become visible.

It distinguishes **first recognizability** from **sustained reality**.

A structure may look real at first contact because it has familiar surface features: fluent language, institutional formatting, photographic appearance, citation form, procedural coherence, or genre-conforming presentation.

But first recognizability is not sustained reality.

Reality consistency requires continued coherence across source, form, scale, exchange, process, and subject-position under progressive scrutiny.

In compressed form:

```text
first recognizability
≠
sustained reality
```

Operationally:

```text
object appears plausible
→ more dimensions become visible
→ source / form / scale / exchange / process / subject-position are checked
→ repair burden is assessed
→ accumulated compatibility or incompatibility determines retention or breakdown
```

What remains real is what continues to hold together.

What breaks down is often not what looked false at first, but what looked real too early.

---

## Core Characteristics

Reality Consistency treats reality retention as a cross-dimensional structural condition rather than a surface property.

Its core characteristics include:

- **First recognizability** — the entry condition in which a structure appears plausible through familiar form, fluency, visual realism, citation style, institutional language, or procedural coherence.
- **Sustained reality** — the condition in which the structure remains coherent as further dimensions become available for evaluation.
- **Source consistency** — the claimed origin, provenance, or authority must support the object, claim, output, or event.
- **Formal consistency** — the form must remain compatible with what the structure claims to be or do.
- **Scale consistency** — the magnitude, confidence, consequence, or scope must fit the evidence and explanation offered.
- **Exchange consistency** — cost, benefit, burden, incentive, and verification labor must remain proportionate and intelligible.
- **Process consistency** — the production path, action sequence, or transformation chain must support the final result.
- **Subject-position consistency** — the role, responsibility, visibility, and capacity assigned to the user, viewer, author, verifier, operator, or institution must remain coherent.
- **Repair burden** — inconsistencies may be repaired, but repair becomes destabilizing when it creates more inconsistency than it resolves.
- **Reality breakdown through accumulation** — failure often emerges when multiple dimensions no longer support one another, not when one isolated detail is wrong.
- **AI-mediated intensification** — generative systems, synthetic media, search summaries, automated documents, and agentic systems can produce high recognizability without equivalent structural support.

Reality Consistency does not treat reality as immediate appearance, subjective certainty, or narrow factual correctness alone.

It treats reality as sustained cross-dimensional coherence under unfolding scrutiny.

## Structural Logic

Reality Consistency follows a recurring structural sequence:

```text
surface plausibility
→ first recognizability
→ progressive unfolding
→ dimensional consistency checking
→ repair burden
→ accumulated compatibility or incompatibility
→ reality retention or breakdown
```

At the entry layer:

```text
the object looks like what it claims to be
the claim sounds plausible
the output is fluent
the image appears photographic
the citation appears authoritative
the report appears institutional
the action trace appears complete
```

At the consistency layer:

```text
source must fit form
form must fit function
scale must fit evidence
exchange must fit burden
process must fit output
subject-position must fit responsibility
```

A structure retains reality status when its dimensions continue to support one another.

A structure breaks down when accumulated incompatibility exceeds what proportional repair can sustain.

Reality breakdown is therefore not always immediate.

It may be gradual, cumulative, and threshold-based.

---

## Distinction

Reality Consistency is not ordinary factual verification.

Factual verification asks whether a claim corresponds to an external fact or record.

Reality Consistency asks whether the structure continues to hold across multiple dimensions as scrutiny expands.

The distinction is structural:

```text
factual verification
= does this claim correspond to a fact or record?

reality consistency
= does this structure remain coherent across source, form, scale, exchange, process, and subject-position?
```

Reality Consistency is also not hallucination detection.

Hallucination detection identifies fabricated or unsupported generated content.

Reality Consistency includes hallucination as one possible failure mode, but it also covers cases where the issue is formal mismatch, scale inflation, process opacity, exchange displacement, or responsibility misalignment.

Reality Consistency is not provenance verification alone.

Provenance verification checks origin, edits, and chain of custody.

Reality Consistency includes provenance under source and process consistency, but also asks whether origin, form, scale, exchange, and subject-position continue to support one another.

Reality Consistency is not plausibility.

Plausibility is an entry condition.

Reality consistency is an endurance condition.

The question is not only:

```text
Can this be accepted initially?
```

The question becomes:

```text
Can this continue to remain coherent after further dimensions appear?
```

## Relevant Interpretation Contexts

Reality Consistency may describe structural conditions across AI-mediated and machine-mediated environments, including:

- AI-generated answers
- synthetic media
- search summaries
- automated legal or administrative drafts
- generated citations
- institutional reports
- agentic AI action traces
- provenance-dependent media objects
- public claims circulating before verification
- machine-mediated transformations of pressure-dense source material

Context relevance does not imply a deployed tool, operational method, audit framework, compliance system, professional service, domain-specific recommendation, or application claim.

In each case, the central issue is not only whether the output appears real, fluent, useful, or plausible.

The issue is whether it remains structurally supportable as its origin, form, scale, exchange logic, production path, and subject-position become visible.

---

## Non-Applicability

Reality Consistency should not be used for every case of doubt.

It does not apply well when:

```text
no progressive scrutiny is possible
no structure needs to remain coherent over time
the object is purely fictional and not presented as structurally real
the analysis concerns only isolated factual correction
the issue is ordinary ambiguity without consistency failure
minor irregularities are being inflated into total invalidation
there is no relation among source, form, scale, exchange, process, and subject-position
```

The model does not claim that every inconsistency falsifies an entire structure.

It names a narrower condition: reality retention or breakdown through accumulated cross-dimensional consistency.

## Naming Declaration

The **Reality Consistency Model** originates within the Meta-Writing Ecology as a structural model for describing how something initially appears real, plausible, or acceptable, yet later fails under accumulated consistency checks.

The term **reality** in this model does not refer only to external factual correctness in the narrow empirical sense. It refers to the condition under which an event, object, sequence, explanation, media artifact, or generated output continues to hold together as sufficiently real, plausible, and internally supportable under ongoing scrutiny.

The term **consistency** does not refer merely to repetition or superficial similarity. It refers to sustained compatibility among source, form, scale, exchange, process, and subject-position as a structure unfolds.

This model is not derived from formal epistemology, hallucination theory, dream interpretation, perception studies, misinformation taxonomies, media forensics, or simple reality-testing frameworks, although intersections may exist.

The model does not claim that reality is purely subjective, nor that every inconsistency falsifies a whole structure.

It defines a narrower structural condition:

```text
a structure loses reality status
when accumulated incompatibilities exceed what the structure can continue to support
```

The Reality Consistency Model therefore asks not only whether something can be recognized at first contact, but what allows it to remain real as further dimensions of it come into view.
